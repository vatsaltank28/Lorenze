04 Process

How it works

What happens from your first message to the final export.

  1. 01

    Submit your brief

    Use the estimator below or message us on WhatsApp, Discord or email with your channel, the video you need and any reference videos.

  2. 02

    Agree scope, price & deadline

    I reply with a quote and next steps — usually within about 10 minutes — we're online 24/7. Payment is upfront; monthly setups start with a free 15-minute call.

  3. 03

    Send footage & assets

    Share your raw footage plus anything the edit needs — references, logos, music or notes.

  4. 04

    Review the draft

    Standard YouTube videos are typically delivered in 2–3 business days; I confirm the exact timeline before starting. Revisions are unlimited.

  5. 05

    Get the final export

    Once you're happy, you receive the approved final export, ready to publish.
